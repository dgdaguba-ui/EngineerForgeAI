# Assembly & post-processing

| Product | Steps | Est. time |
|---|---|---|
| CRW-001 keyring | remove from plate, check hole is clear, fit 25 mm split ring + chain link | 0.5 min + 0.3 post |
| CRW-002 magnet | press two 12 x 3 mm magnets into the back pockets (flush); optional drop of CA glue | 0.6 min + 0.3 post |
| CRW-003 mini cow | remove from plate; no supports; inspect horn tips | 1.0 min post |
| CRW-004 phone stand | slide the four TPU pads into their dovetail grooves from the side (rear wall, front lip, two feet); trim flush if needed | 1.5 min + 1.0 post |
| CRW-005 articulated cow | break each joint free by gently rotating head/legs through their range 3-4 times; flex the TPU tail | 3.0 min post |

Pad positions (CRW-004): the pad objects are named `tpu_pad_rear_wall`, `tpu_pad_front_lip`, `tpu_pad_foot_rear`,
`tpu_pad_foot_front`; all four are identical, so any pad fits any groove.
