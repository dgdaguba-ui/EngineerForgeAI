# CRW-003 - Cowa Mini Collectible - No.01 Classic

*A/B - Mini collectible (Cowaramup Cow Collection) - STANDARD - tier SMALL_GIFT*

Sculpted sitting Cowa: head turned to camera, relaxed lids, lopsided smile, split hooves, raised organic patches. Stands on its own; 3 colours.

![four-colour](../../previews/CRW-003-mini-cow-classic/2-four-colour.png)

## Manufacturing

- **Strategy:** A - one multi-colour model (sculpted 3D colour volumes)
- **Print orientation:** Upright as displayed. No supports (45-degree closure is built into the model).
- **Plate footprint:** 41.23 x 42.76 x 61.39 mm
- **Supports:** required
- **Hardware:** none
- **Recommended batch:** 16 per plate

## Self-critique

- **character:** Head turn + roll, relaxed lids, lopsided smile, asymmetric ears -> reads as a personality.
- **exploits 4 tools:** Deluxe: 4 colours used for storytelling (paddock/collar/bell/tag) not decoration.
- **still to improve:** Patch edges are procedural-organic; a hand-drawn patch set per series cow would add more individuality. Back view is simpler than the front.

## Notes / known risks

- Chin, ear and bell overhangs carry moulded 45-degree fillets from the support-free closure.
- Eye highlights are >= 1 mm radius at every size (3.4 mm^2 islands).

## Toolhead set-up issues

- [WARN] colour Earth Brown is not commonly available in TPU
