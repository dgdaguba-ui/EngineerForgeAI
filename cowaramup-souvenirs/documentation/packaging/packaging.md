# Packaging

Mock-up: `previews/packaging-card-keyring.png` (60 x 100 mm backing card, hang hole, product window).

## Card copy (only factually true claims)

```
COWARAMUP
COW TOWN  WA
4-Colour 3D Printed Souvenir
Original design
```

- "4-Colour 3D Printed" is true: every prototype prints with four tools, no paint.
- Do **not** use "Made in Cowaramup" / "Locally made" unless printing actually happens there.
- Do not name filament brands or claim "eco", "biodegradable" or "food-safe".
- Magnet card: add "Contains magnets - keep away from small children" (small neodymium magnets).
- Articulated cow: small parts / not for children under 3 (joints and TPU tail).

## By tier (costs in config/costs.json -> packaging)

| Tier | Products | Pack |
|---|---|---|
| Impulse | keyring, magnet | backing card, product visible, hang hole |
| Small gift | mini cow, phone stand | card + clear bag; mini standing on its plinth |
| Premium | articulated cow | window box posed "walking", card insert explaining it was printed fully assembled |
| Collectible | B-series | uniform collector box, numbered series strip "COLLECT THEM ALL" |

Design around the colours: neutral card (cream) behind the product so white/black/pink read; Australian green
header band carries the town name in gold.
