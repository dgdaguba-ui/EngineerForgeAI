## Rack plan (printable with the same system later - concepts, not yet modelled)

| Rack | Holds | Colour coding |
|---|---|---|
| Keyring peg board | 6 variants x 4 pegs | peg tips in the variant's tool-4 colour |
| Magnet steel strip board | 9-12 magnets | green header |
| Mini cow step riser | 3 steps x 6 minis | earth-brown steps (matches plinths) |
| Premium shelf | articulated cows posed walking + phone stand with a phone | cream background |

Layout principle: colour variants side by side (same geometry, different colours) makes the multi-colour
capability obvious from 2-3 m; one articulated cow is kept as a hands-on demo.
