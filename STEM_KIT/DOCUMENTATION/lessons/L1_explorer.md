# Level 1 — Explorer (ages ≈ 7–9)

Goal: children discover that machines are made of a few simple parts that
they can take apart, rebuild and change — and that "it doesn't work" is the
start of the fun. No formulas; count, compare, feel.

**Every lesson follows the loop:** BUILD → TEST → FAIL → MODIFY → TEST AGAIN → UNDERSTAND.

---

## Lesson 1.1 — The magic grid (45 min)
**Parts:** base plate, rails, corner connectors, thumb nuts, M3 × 10 screws.
**Big idea:** machines are built on a pattern; if you follow the pattern, parts fit.

1. Count the holes along one edge (10). Find the hole in the very middle.
2. Build a "wall" standing up with two corner connectors. Screw from the top,
   nut underneath — no spanner needed, the nut can't spin in its pocket.
3. Try to screw a corner connector *between* two holes. Why won't it go?
4. Join two plates with a rail. Do the holes still line up across the joint?

**Talk about it:** Why do LEGO bricks, city streets and graph paper all use grids?

## Lesson 1.2 — Gears are wheels with teeth (45 min)
**Parts:** gearbox frame, 10T, 20T, 40T gears, shafts.
1. Put a 10T and a 40T on shafts 50 mm apart (the diagonal!). Turn the big one
   once — count the small one using the triangle marker (4 turns).
2. Which one is easier to turn by hand? Which one is faster?
3. Put the shafts one hole too close. What happens? One hole too far?
4. Add a third gear in the middle. Which way does the last gear turn now?

**Talk about it:** a bicycle has gears too — which gear do you use to go up a hill?

## Lesson 1.3 — Wheels and axles (30 min)
**Parts:** solar chassis, axle mounts, wheels, O-ring tyres, steel axles.
1. Build a rolling chassis (no motor). Push it down a ramp.
2. Remove the O-ring tyres. Does it roll further or less far? Does it grip?
3. Loosen one wheel's set screw. What happens when it rolls?

## Lesson 1.4 — Sunshine makes it move (45 min)
**Parts:** solar vehicle version B.
1. Take the car into the sun. Shade half the panel with your hand. Then all of it.
2. Tilt the panel toward the sun and away. When is the car fastest?
3. Race version A against version B. Surprise?

**Talk about it:** where does the energy come from? Where does it go?

## Lesson 1.5 — Make your own electricity (45 min)
**Parts:** hand-crank dynamo (guard on!), output panel with LED and buzzer.
1. Turn the crank slowly, then fast. What does the LED do?
2. Try 1:1 and 8:1. Which one lights the LED? Which one is harder to turn?
3. Disconnect the LED: is the crank easier to turn? (Yes — you are not making
   as much electricity, so you do less work.)

## Lesson 1.6 — Belts and pulleys (30 min)
Count turns of a small pulley driving a big one. Cross the belt — watch the
direction flip. Loosen the belt until it slips.

## Explorer badge challenge
Build any machine that moves something 30 cm using only kit parts, and tell
the class which part you changed three times before it worked.
