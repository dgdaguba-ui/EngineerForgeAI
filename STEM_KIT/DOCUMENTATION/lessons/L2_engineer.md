# Level 2 — Engineer (ages ≈ 9–12)

Goal: measure, calculate simple ratios, draw energy chains, connect circuits.
Children predict with numbers, test, and explain the difference.

---

## Lesson 2.1 — Gear ratio as a number (60 min)
* ratio = teeth(driven) ÷ teeth(driver); compound trains multiply.
* The **grid rule**: with module-2 gears, meshing shafts are (z1 + z2) mm
  apart. Check it with a ruler on the frame plate.
* Activity: Experiment E06. Predict every ratio, then count. Record percentage
  error: (measured − predicted) ÷ predicted.
* Extension: speed × force stays about the same — hang a small weight from a
  string wound on the output shaft and find the smallest input force.

## Lesson 2.2 — Mechanical advantage and leverage (45 min)
* Crank at 20 mm vs 30 mm radius: which needs less force for the same
  generator load? Torque = force × radius.
* Use a luggage scale or a rubber band "force meter" on the knob.

## Lesson 2.3 — Circuits: open, closed, series, parallel (60 min)
* Connect the generator to one LED, then two in series, then two in parallel.
* Use the multimeter: DC volts across the LED, DC milliamps in series.
* Why does an LED only work one way round? (Diode — polarity.)
* Capacitor: charge it with the crank, then let it run the LED. Count seconds.

## Lesson 2.4 — Generators: a motor running backwards (60 min)
* Spin a motor by hand with a coupler and shaft; measure voltage.
* Experiment E02 at 1:1, 2:1, 4:1, 8:1. Plot voltage against generator rpm —
  a straight line! (V = k × rpm.) Find k from your graph.
* Why the kit uses a 12 V-rated motor as generator: it has a bigger k.

## Lesson 2.5 — Turbines: wind and blade pitch (60 min)
Experiment E03 parts 1–3. Graph voltage against pitch angle. Find the peak.

## Lesson 2.6 — Solar power and angle (45 min)
Experiment E07. Graph current against tilt. Where is the peak? Why does the
curve look like a hill?

## Lesson 2.7 — Energy chains (30 min)
Draw the chain for every kit, and label where energy is **lost** (heat in the
motor, friction in bushings, sound of gears, belt slip).
Solar car: light → electrical → magnetic/kinetic (motor) → gears → wheels → motion + heat + sound.

## Engineer badge challenge
Design a gear train for the solar car that climbs a 3-book ramp. Present:
prediction, drawing, measured result, and one thing that surprised you.
