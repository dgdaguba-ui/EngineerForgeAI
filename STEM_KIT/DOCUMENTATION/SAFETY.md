# Safety

STEM_KIT is designed for children aged about 7–14 **with adult supervision**.

## Designed-in

| Hazard | What the design does |
|---|---|
| Sharp edges | Every exposed edge is rounded or chamfered (`edge_r`, `chamfer`); blades, links and guard bars keep ≥ 2 mm thick edges (`min_rim_thickness`). Button-head screws only. |
| Small parts | The smallest printed parts (shaft collar Ø14, knob, pins of the tolerance test) and all hardware (M3 nuts, grub screws) are **choking hazards: not for children under 3**, and younger children need supervision. |
| Pinch points in gears | The hand-crank gearbox is closed by a see-through guard (openings < 5 mm). Large gears live inside the gearbox frame, whose lattice openings are < 6.2 mm. The crank knob spins freely, so it cannot twist skin. |
| Crank near the table | The crank radius is limited to 30 mm with the shaft 55 mm above the base, leaving > 15 mm between knob and base plate (no finger trap). |
| Rotating blades | Thick, round-edged, flat PLA blades; run the fan on low, stay out of the rotor plane and an arm's length away, stop the rotor by switching the fan off. |
| Fast parts | Vehicles and turbines run from ≤ 1 W sources; no high-speed exposed parts beyond the wind rotor. |
| Electricity | Everything is **≤ 12 V DC** (solar panel 5 V, hand generator < 12 V, AA packs). **Nothing in the kit connects to mains electricity, and nothing may be modified to do so.** |
| Water (future water turbine) | Designed for tap flow (< 0.5 bar) and a splash housing; keep electronics above and away from water. |

## Rules for the classroom

1. Adults cut steel rods and deburr them; children never use cutters.
2. Wear eye protection when running the wind turbine, when snapping motors
   into mounts and when working with springs or stretched O-rings.
3. The guard stays on the dynamo gearbox while it is being cranked.
4. LEDs and capacitors: observe polarity; never short a capacitor or battery.
   Super-capacitors can deliver high current — use the 1 F / 5.5 V type listed
   and discharge it through the LED, not a wire.
5. Keep hair and loose sleeves away from turning shafts.
6. Printed parts can have sharp strings or elephant-foot edges straight off the
   printer: an adult checks and deburrs every part before handing it out.
7. Solar experiments outdoors: never look at the sun, no magnifying lenses.
